## Single Room PG in Bangalore: What to Ask {#single-room-price}

A single room, meaning a private room for one person, is priced separately from the starting rate you see on listings. The starting rate is usually a bed in a 2 or 3 sharing room, so expect a single room in the same building to cost noticeably more.

Single room prices aren't consistently published, and they change with availability, so ask the operator directly. Before you pay, confirm:

- **That the room is actually private.** Some listings use "single sharing" to mean one bed in a shared room.
- **The exact single room rate, in writing,** including what it does and doesn't cover (food, electricity, AC).
- **Availability.** Private rooms often go to existing residents who upgrade first, and some operators only show room types that are currently free.

If budget matters, start in the areas with the lowest starting rates (Marathahalli, Whitefield, Electronic City), where a single room is most likely to stay within a mid-range budget.

## PG Cost in Bangalore With Food vs Without Food {#food}

Food is the biggest cost that the headline rent can hide.

- **Which operators include food:** Stanza Living properties typically list meals as an amenity. Zolo varies by building: some list lunch, while Zolo's own pages for Zeppelin, Lakefront and Lumina in Bellandur say food is not available. HelloWorld's site says meals are not included at every HelloWorld home we checked, which have shared kitchens instead. Confirm the plan with the operator.
- **If food isn't included:** published Bangalore cost-of-living estimates put food at roughly ₹3,000 to ₹6,000 a month for one person eating through tiffin, a mess or groceries, more if you order in often. A mess subscription near a college campus can start at around ₹3,000.
- **Self cooking PGs:** a smaller number of PGs give residents kitchen access. These suit people with dietary needs (pure veg, Jain, Kerala-style or North Indian food) who would rather cook than adjust to a common menu.

**The rule of thumb:** compare the rent difference between a PG with and without meals against the ₹3,000 to ₹6,000 you would spend on food yourself. If you eat two meals a day at home, a PG with meals usually works out cheaper. If you eat at the office cafeteria, a PG without food can save you money.

Most branded co-living menus serve South Indian breakfasts and North Indian dinners (roti, dal, sabzi). If you need a specific diet, ask for the weekly menu before booking.

Working women's hostels and ladies PGs overlap, but they work differently.

- **Trust-run and association working women's hostels** (such as the YWCA) usually offer shared rooms of 2 to 5 beds, with fixed entry times, waiting lists and stricter visitor rules. Most do not publish fees online, so call the hostel for current rates and mess charges. Some hostels elsewhere in India link the fee to the resident's salary.

- **Private ladies PGs** run by local landlords vary widely in price and rules, so compare them against the managed rates above.

**Ladies PG rent vs gents PG rent:** in managed co-living, rent is set by room type, not gender. ## Hidden Costs and Scams That Inflate Your Rent {#scams}

The headline rent is rarely the full monthly cost. Before you compare two PGs on price, check these:

- **Electricity:** some PGs include it, others bill it separately. Zolo's pages for Zeppelin and Lakefront in Bellandur, for example, say electricity is billed on actual usage. Ask which, and ask for the per-unit rate. With AC in summer, a separate electricity bill can add ₹1,000 or more a month.
- **Maintenance or service fee:** a fixed monthly charge some landlords add on top of rent.
- **One-time joining or onboarding fee:** some operators charge a few hundred to a few thousand rupees on move-in. Ask for it upfront, because it is rarely shown in the headline rate.
- **The starting rate itself:** the advertised "from" price is the cheapest room available right now, often a bed in a 3 sharing room. At Zolo Cinnamon in Marathahalli, for example, the ₹4,898 starting rate is for three sharing.
- **Deposit deductions:** painting and cleaning deductions are near-universal in local PGs. Take a timestamped video walkthrough on move-in day and share it over WhatsApp so it is on record.
- **Lock-in penalties:** leaving before the lock-in ends can mean losing part or all of the deposit.
- **Last-mile commute:** if the nearest metro or your office is not walkable, budget ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 a month for autos.

### The 3 Scams to Dodge

The market is flooded with fake listings designed to prey on newcomers. According to recent reports from the [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/bengaluru-techies-sharp-instincts-save-him-from-flat-rental-fraud-know-how/articleshow/115799681.cms), cybercrime complaints regarding rental fraud have risen sharply.

**1. The "Visiting Charge" Scam.** You see a stunning room online at an unbelievably low price. When you call, the "owner" asks for ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 as a "Gate Pass" or "Visiting Token". Once you pay via UPI, they block your number. Never pay money just to see a property.

**2. The "Fake Listing" Bait.** Scammers steal photos from hotels or high-end apartments and list them as PGs on classified sites. If a fully furnished room in Koramangala is listed at ₹8,000 and looks like a 5-star hotel, it is a scam.

**3. The "Processing Fee" Trap.** Some brokers ask for a "booking token" to block a room before you land in the city, then claim it is "unavailable" and refuse to refund the token, citing processing charges.

## Before You Move In: The Checklist Bengaluru PG Hunters Always Skip

- **Water supply:** ask whether it is Cauvery-fed or tanker-dependent. SG Palya, Bellandur and parts of Whitefield rely heavily on tankers. Ask: "Is 24/7 water guaranteed even in May?"
- **Power backup:** ask what it covers. Many PGs advertise "power backup" but mean lights and fans only. If you work from home, confirm your laptop, router and AC are on the backup circuit.
- **Parking:** if you have a bike or car, confirm covered or assigned parking before assuming it is included.
- **AC:** with Bangalore summers getting hotter, AC is now on most professionals' checklist, and it changes the rent.
- **Noise:** visit on a weekday evening, not a Sunday afternoon. Construction in HSR, Marathahalli and Whitefield can run 10 to 12 hours a day.
- **Internet speed:** test it at 9 PM, when shared connections are most congested.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faqs}

**Q: What is the average PG rent in Bangalore?**

There is no reliable city-wide average. In our October 2026 check of 109 managed listings from Zolo, Stanza Living and HelloWorld, the median starting rate was ₹9,999. Local landlord PGs aren't included and can be cheaper. Marathahalli (₹6,613), Whitefield (₹7,440) and Electronic City (₹7,554) had the lowest medians, and HSR Layout (₹13,448) and Indiranagar (₹14,572) the highest.

**Q: What is the rent for a single room PG in Bangalore?**

Single rooms are priced separately from the starting rates shown on listings and vary by building and availability, so ask the operator for the exact rate. Starting rates for shared rooms in October 2026 ranged from ₹4,797 in Electronic City to ₹35,000 in HSR Layout.

**Q: How much does a PG in Bangalore cost with food?**

Stanza Living properties usually list meals as an amenity. Their starting rates ranged from ₹6,499 to ₹19,399 in our October 2026 data. If your PG doesn't include food, published cost-of-living estimates put food at about ₹3,000 to ₹6,000 a month for one person.

**Q: What is the rent for a PG in Bangalore per day?**

Most PGs in Bangalore don't publish daily prices. Some co-living operators accept short stays on request, priced individually. For stays of a month or more, monthly pricing is always much cheaper than paying by the day.

**Q: Do PGs in Bangalore allow couples?**

Most PGs are gender-segregated by room or floor, so couple-friendly options are limited. A few studio-style co-living setups allow couples, but confirm the policy in writing before you pay anything.

**Q: Which is the cheapest area for PG in Bangalore?**

Marathahalli had the lowest median starting rate in our October 2026 data (₹6,613), followed by Whitefield (₹7,440) and Electronic City (₹7,554). Electronic City has the most budget supply overall, with 30 managed listings, half of them between ₹7,181 and ₹8,438.

**Q: How much deposit do PGs in Bangalore charge?**

Managed co-living operators usually charge 1 to 2 months of rent with a documented refund policy. Local landlord PGs typically charge 1 to 3 months, often informally.

**Q: Are working women's hostels cheaper than ladies PGs in Bangalore?**

Often, but most trust-run hostels such as the YWCA don't publish their fees, so call for current rates and mess charges. They also come with waiting lists and stricter entry times. Managed co-living has published prices: in October 2026, starting rates ran from ₹4,797 in Electronic City to a median of ₹14,572 in Indiranagar.

**Q: Is co-living the same as a PG in Bangalore?**

Not exactly. A [traditional PG](https://instadwell.com/blog/what-is-paying-guest-house-a-complete-guide-to-paying-guest-accommodation-in-india) is typically run by a private landlord with basic amenities. Co-living spaces are run by professional operators with standardized amenities and app-based maintenance, and usually cost 20 to 40% more.
