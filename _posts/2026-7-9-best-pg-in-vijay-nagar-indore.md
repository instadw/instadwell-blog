## Who should live in Vijay Nagar

Students at Prestige, Vaishnav or Acropolis benefit most from Vijay Nagar's walkability, and several properties, including HelloWorld Fairy and Minya House, sit close enough to campus that a daily commute isn't really a factor. Working professionals at TCS or the offices around C21 Mall tend to prioritise a slightly higher rent for better furnishing and security, which is where Easton House and HelloWorld Macao fit in. First time renters moving to Indore generally do well starting in a managed property rather than an independent room, since deposit terms, food and housekeeping are agreed upfront rather than added on later.

## Safety tips before you book

Never pay a full deposit before physically visiting the room or verifying it through a call with the resident manager. Ask for the exact floor, sharing type and whether AC and electricity are billed separately before you commit. If you are booking for a woman, confirm the warden's presence in person, not just in the listing description. Confirm the notice period and deposit refund policy in writing, verbally agreed terms are the most common source of disputes at move out.
