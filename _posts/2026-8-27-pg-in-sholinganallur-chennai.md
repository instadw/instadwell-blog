## PG With Food or Without Food

Food arrangements vary by property, so check the meal plan before booking rather than assuming it matches the rent you've seen elsewhere. Some PGs in the area are strictly vegetarian, some include non-veg on certain days, and others operate without an in-house mess at all.

Before booking, confirm:

- Whether breakfast, lunch, and dinner are included in the rent
- Whether vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals are both available
- How many days a week non-veg is served, and whether it's cooked separately
- Whether outside food or in-room cooking is allowed

Compare the total monthly cost, food included, rather than just the advertised room rent.

## Check This Before You Book

Sholinganallur sits close to the Pallikaranai marsh, and flooding can vary significantly from one street to another during heavy monsoon rain, particularly around lower-lying parts of the wider Sholinganallur, Semmencheri, and Perumbakkam belt.

Before you book, ask people already living in or near that specific PG whether the building or street floods during monsoon. A five-minute conversation with a current resident will tell you more than any map or listing ever will.
