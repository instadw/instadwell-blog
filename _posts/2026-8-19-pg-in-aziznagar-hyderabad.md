## Things to Check Before Choosing a PG in Aziznagar

PG quality in Aziznagar varies from property to property, so the checklist matters more than the location name. Before you book a PG in Aziznagar, verify.

1. Room occupancy and actual space. Ask for current photos, not just listing photos, especially for shared rooms.
2. Food arrangement. Meal timings, what is included, and whether portions run out during peak hours.
3. Water quality. Ask about this directly before you move in.
4. Responsiveness of management. Ask how maintenance requests are handled before you move in, not after.
5. Deposit amount and lock in period. Get this in writing.
6. Notice period and refund terms.
7. Electricity charges and maintenance charges. Clarify what is metered separately.
8. Visitor policy and gate closing time.
9. Wi-Fi speed and reliability. This matters more if you work from home occasionally.

A few properties in any given area consistently perform better than the rest on these points. The goal is identifying which ones, not assuming every PG in Aziznagar has the same issues. If you are comparing PG hostels in Aziznagar with options in Gachibowli or Kondapur, the main trade off is lower rent versus commute time.
