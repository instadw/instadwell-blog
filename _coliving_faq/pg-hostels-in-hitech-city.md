PG hostels in Hitech City start from Rs. 6520 at Zolo Quest, the cheapest verified listing in the area, going up to Rs. 15199 at Stanza Living Vigo House at the premium end. Unlike an independent, landlord run PG hostel, every property in this pocket is managed by an operator, either Zolo, HelloWorld or Stanza Living, so pricing typically includes housekeeping and, at several properties, meals as standard rather than an add on. HelloWorld Kohinoor in Serilingampally is the most affordable option with kitchen access for residents who prefer cooking their own food. Confirm sharing type and meal inclusion directly with the operator, since starting rent reflects the cheapest bed in the building.
